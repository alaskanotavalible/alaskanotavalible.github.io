Privacy Policy — Azhar Assistant

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Azhar Assistant is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission via Google OAuth, the app reads, sends and deletes messages in the owner's own Gmail account in order to summarize new emails and perform actions the owner explicitly confirms.

How data is used

Email content is processed only to provide summaries and replies to the owner in Telegram. Text may be sent to OpenAI's API to generate summaries. Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Storage

The app runs on the owner's own computer (self-hosted n8n). Emails are not stored permanently by the app; OAuth tokens are stored locally in encrypted form.

Google API Services

Azhar Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
